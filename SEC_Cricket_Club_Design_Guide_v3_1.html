shield01 — Brand Identity

Color System

The SEC color system is extracted directly from the club crest: Deep Navy #1A2744 carries authority and brotherhood; Crimson Red #C41230 delivers energy and competitive spirit; Silver/Steel adds precision and prestige. These three replace any green-based palette across all app surfaces.

warning
Brand Alignment

All previous design iterations using green as the primary colour are deprecated. The SEC crest — crossed cricket bats, shield, and crimson ribbon — defines navy + crimson + silver as the canonical brand palette. No deviations.

text_fields02 — Foundations

Typography

Inter is the sole UI typeface — geometric, neutral, built for screens, with 9 weights for expressive hierarchy without ever feeling informal. JetBrains Mono is reserved strictly for code tokens and numeric data. No display font switching — Inter at 800–900 weight delivers all the headline power needed.

blur_on04 — Glassmorphism

Glass Surfaces

Glass is the primary surface language of the SEC app on all dark backgrounds — the navy-dark app bar, the navy card headers, event header banners, the profile hero, and the sponsor showcase. Two glass variants: standard white-tinted glass and red-tinted glass for action-focused surfaces. Glass is never used on the white card body or neutral screen backgrounds.

grid_view08 — Icons

Material Icons Round

All SEC app icons use Material Icons Round. Standard size: 24px in all buttons, nav, and toolbars. 18px inline with text. 48px for empty states only. Navy primary for standard icons, Crimson for action/alert icons, Silver for inactive states.

warning
No Emoji as Icons

All UI icons must be Material Icons Round. Emoji are only permitted in user-generated content such as announcement body text. Never in buttons, nav, chips, or list icons.
